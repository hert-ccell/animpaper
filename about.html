WHO WE ARE

About AnimPaper

AnimPaper is an independent wallpaper discovery project built for anime fans. Our goal is to make it easy to find attractive backgrounds for phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Artwork and ownership

We aim to publish original artwork and content that we have permission to share. Anime titles and character names belong to their respective rights holders. AnimPaper is not affiliated with or endorsed by anime studios or publishers.

Using the website

Downloads are provided for personal wallpaper use unless a specific license says otherwise. Please check each artwork's credit and license before reusing, redistributing or using it commercially.
